About

I am an undergraduate in Electronic Information Engineering at Dalian University of Technology and a research intern at Westlake University's ENCODE Lab, supervised by Huan Wang. Previously, I worked with Shuai Yu in Huchuan Lu's IIAU Lab at Dalian University of Technology.

My research focuses on multimodal learning and reasoning and continual learning. I work on audio-visual consistency, evidence-grounded deepfake detection, and distribution shifts during continual training. I am also interested in software engineering and efficient AI systems.

I plan to pursue a PhD starting in 2027.

Papers & Manuscripts

  1. HIM-CoT: Reasoning over the Human-Instrument-Music Chain for Musical Performance Deepfake Detection

    Shuai Yu*, Xianrui Hou*, Yue Zhu, Yuecheng Zhuo, Li Wang, Lijun Wang, Yongqi Shan, Huchuan Lu

    Under review at ICLR 2027 · * Equal contribution

    A framework for reasoning over audio-visual evidence, evaluated on musical-performance deepfakes. HIM-CoT achieves 89.3% accuracy and 95.0% AP on HIM-DF.

    I co-designed the framework, built the benchmark and supervision datasets, and co-developed the evaluation pipeline.

  2. Learning What to Execute and When: Reasoning Protocols for LLM Inference

    Yu Huo, Kun Zeng, Siyu Zhang, Haoyue Liu, Yuecheng Zhuo, Xianrui Hou, YuQuan Lu, Xiaoying Tang

    Under review at ICLR 2027

    The proposed framework, REPROT, learns which reasoning operations to execute and when to allocate extra computation. Across eight benchmarks, it achieves 84.2% average accuracy at 3.9 calls per question.

    I reproduced baselines, evaluated experimental results, and analyzed accuracy–cost trade-offs.
